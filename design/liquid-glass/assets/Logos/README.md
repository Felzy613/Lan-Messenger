# Logos

The LAN Messenger mark: two overlapping speech bubbles with a typing ellipsis over the LAN MESSENGER wordmark, in a blue-to-cyan gradient on a near-black ground. Both files are copied unchanged from the repository.

- `lan-messenger-logo.png` (1254 × 1254, from `Images/Logo.png`) is the full mark, for the README, release pages and About panels.
- `app-icon-512.png` (512 × 512, from the macOS `AppIcon.appiconset`) is the app icon as it appears in the Dock, taskbar and installers.

The mark keeps its own blue-cyan palette and dark ground. Don't recolour it WhatsApp green, and don't set it on glass: place it on `ink` or on its own dark square. The interface's green (`brand`) and the mark's blue are deliberately separate, the way a messenger's icon and its bubbles differ.
